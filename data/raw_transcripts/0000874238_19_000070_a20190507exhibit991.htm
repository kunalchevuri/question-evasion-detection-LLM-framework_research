Historically, our first quarter has been our slowest seasonal quarter, and this year was no exception. Versus prior year first quarter, our revenues were up slightly driven by a 21% increase in our residential business. Our net income was down $700,000 driven by over $1.5 million of weather-related inefficiencies and $1.5 million of incremental earn-out in members' interest expenses.
