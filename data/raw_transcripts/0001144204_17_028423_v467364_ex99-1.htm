We believe that general and administrative expenses should fall in the short-term, as certain cost reduction initiatives are fully deployed during the second half of 2017, with a portion of those starting in the second quarter of 2017.

 

As we've discussed previously, we plan to see reductions in annualized operating expenses in 2017 commencing in the second quarter of around $0.5 million annualized and growing to around $2.5 million to $3 million in overall annualized savings as we enter 2018.
