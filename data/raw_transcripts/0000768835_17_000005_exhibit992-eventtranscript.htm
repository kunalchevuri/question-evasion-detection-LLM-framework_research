As a reminder, Q4 2016 included two new significant costs, PSUs and e-commerce, the net impact of which was approximately $0.10 to the quarter. Moving on to the balance sheet, inventory ended fiscal 2016 at $859 million compared to $850 million last year. Inventory levels per store were up 2%, offset by a lower store count year over year. As I mentioned earlier, we are pleased with our content as we begin the new year.

Capital expenditures in fiscal 2016 were $90 million compared to $126 million last year and depreciation expense was $120 million, a decrease of $2 million to last year. Cash flow defined as cash provided by operating activities less cash used in investing activities was $227 million, exceeding our updated outlook of $195 million primarily due to improved accounts payable leverage, lower CapEx and the gain on sale of real estate noted in Andy's opening comments.

The entirety of our cash flow was returned to you our shareholders. We ended fiscal 2016 with $51 million of cash and cash equivalents and $106 million of borrowings under our credit facility.
