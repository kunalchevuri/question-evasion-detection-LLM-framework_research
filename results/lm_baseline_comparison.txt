Note: the fitted LOO-CV models perform no better than, and often
worse than, a single raw LM proportion. With n=75 and 6-7 features
there is not enough signal in the lexical features to fit against,
so cross-validation correctly exposes the fit as noise. This is
evidence the dictionary approach lacks signal here, not evidence of
an under-tuned baseline.
